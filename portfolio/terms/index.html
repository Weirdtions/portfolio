Project agreement · Draft template

Clear terms.
Clear expectations.

This page provides a structure for discussing a commission. It is a draft to personalize, not an active service agreement. Confirm the final terms directly before starting work.

On this page

Scope of work →Assets & access →Payment & milestones →Delivery & review →Questions →
01 / Agreement outline

Scope of work

Write down what the system does.

A project brief should describe the included features, supported devices, required integrations and acceptance criteria. Confirm that document before work begins.

Changes belong in writing.

New features or changes in direction should be discussed together with their impact on the quote and schedule.

02 / Agreement outline

Assets & access

Identify what is needed to begin.

List the models, animations, effects, sounds, UI designs and access permissions required by the project. Agree on who provides each item and when it will be available.

Keep responsibilities clear.

Asset production, rigging, existing-code repairs and new integrations should be discussed explicitly when they are part of the work.

03 / Agreement outline

Payment & milestones

Confirm the details directly.

Price, currency, payment method and milestone arrangements must be agreed directly for each project. This sample page does not set rates or request payment.

Agree before paying.

Use a written agreement covering the scope, payment schedule and applicable cancellation arrangements.

04 / Agreement outline

Delivery & review

Use the brief to review the result.

Agree on the delivery format, testing environment and review process. Review the behavior against the written requirements and record any remaining issues clearly.

Define the handoff.

Confirm documentation, configuration guidance, ownership and any support period as part of the project agreement.

Before starting

Anything unclear?

Bring questions to the conversation before agreeing to a project.
