Practical answers about project scope, pricing, delivery and the examples in this portfolio.

Scope & process

4 questions
How do I start a conversation?

Send a short description of your game and the systems you want to discuss. Include your references, the current state of the project and any deadlines.

What should a project brief include?

Describe the behavior of each feature, the supported devices, any existing systems it must connect to and the assets that are already available.

Can a reference game explain the whole scope?

A reference can communicate a direction, but a written feature list is still useful. Spell out the interactions and rules that matter to your version.

What happens if the scope changes?

Discuss new requirements before development continues so that the scope, quote and schedule can be updated together.

Pricing & delivery

4 questions
How much does a system cost?

A price depends on the specific behavior, complexity and integrations. No rates have been configured in this sample portfolio; discuss the scope to receive a project-specific quote.

Is there a fixed delivery time?

Delivery dates are agreed individually. Feature scope, asset readiness and the state of the existing project all affect the schedule.

Which payment methods are available?

Payment methods, currency and milestone arrangements should be confirmed directly before accepting a commission. This template does not establish payment terms.

How should revisions be handled?

Agree in advance on the review process, the acceptance criteria and how revisions differ from additional features. Put those details in the written agreement.

Technical details

4 questions
Are both client and server systems included?

The service examples cover both. The exact division of responsibilities and the systems included in your project belong in its written scope.

Who provides models, animations and effects?

Identify the assets and their owners in the brief. Scripting and asset production are separate requirements that need to be planned together.

Do the examples represent shipped commissions?

No. The current project entries demonstrate the portfolio layout and technical breakdown format. Their preview artwork is credited to the reference site.

Can I ask about an existing project?

Yes. Describe the current setup and the changes you want to discuss. Sharing the relevant structure and a reproducible problem helps make the conversation concrete.

Have a GDD for your game?

Send it over on Discord. We can go through the scope together.
